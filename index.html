WHY YOUR RESUME MATTERS

Make your qualifications easy to spot.

Principals and HR review many applications. Picture an 8–10 second first glance: can they see why you fit this role and school?

Your resume highlights the skills, accomplishments, and qualifications that make you a strong fit. It isn’t a list of everything you’ve done or every job duty.

YOUR GOAL

Make every detail help the school see your fit.
